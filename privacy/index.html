Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Elg is a film camera for iPhone. It doesn’t collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app doesn’t use any third-party SDKs.

This policy covers the Elg iPhone app and the elg.pics website. “Elg”, “we” and “us” mean the makers of Elg.

Your photos and settings stay on your iPhone

Everything you make in Elg is stored on your device: your photos, rolls, Films, darkroom edits and settings. The app doesn’t send any of it to us or anyone else, and we can’t see it. Elg’s folder also shows up in the Files app so you can get to your originals.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, that backup may include Elg’s files, under Apple’s terms. If you delete Elg, its files are removed from your iPhone, except for photos you’ve already saved to your Photos library or exported.

Permissions

Elg only asks for what it needs, and only uses each one on your iPhone:

  • Camera — to take photos.
  • Photos (add only) — to save developed photos to your library. Elg can’t see or browse your library.
  • Location (optional, off by default) — if you turn on location tags, Elg adds where you shot to new photos while the camera is open.
  • Motion — to show the optional camera level. Motion readings aren’t saved or shared.

You can change any of these at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Location

Location tags are off unless you turn them on. When they’re on, the location is saved inside each new photo’s file on your iPhone, including copies Elg saves to your Photos library. When you export or share photos from Elg, the location is removed unless you choose to include it. Exporting a RAW original that has a location tag keeps it.

Sharing and exporting

Nothing leaves Elg unless you share or export it yourself. When you do:

  • Photos carry standard camera details such as the date, exposure settings and device model, and can include the settings of the Film used to develop them.
  • Film links and cards contain the Film’s name and settings, its card colour, and a signature if one was saved with it. Film links open straight in the app and don’t go through our servers.

Where your shared photos and Films go after that is up to you and the app you share them with.

Purchases

Elg Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your payment details or Apple ID. The app checks your subscription status with Apple and keeps a small record of it on your iPhone so Pro works offline. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple account settings.

Information from Apple

If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics that don’t identify you. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Website

elg.pics is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, so those services receive your IP address and browser details when you visit, under their own privacy policies. We don’t use cookies or analytics on the site.

If you join the waitlist, the form sends your email address to us through Formspree. We use it only to tell you when Elg launches. Email us and we’ll remove it.

Children

Elg isn’t directed at children under 13. Because the app doesn’t collect personal data, we don’t knowingly collect it from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version here and update the effective date. If a change means Elg starts collecting data, we’ll say so clearly in the app before it happens.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@elg.pics.
